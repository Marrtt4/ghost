Ghostgleam

You're a lonely little ghost drifting through a haunted graveyard. Point with your mouse or finger and you float after it. Gobble candy for combos, but zombies, bats and charging pumpkin brutes are hunting you. Click, tap or press Space to BOO: a shockwave that stuns and shoves monsters away. Grab a glowing jack-o'-lantern to flip the tables and chase them down.
